A Collection source plays every item in the collection, with series expanded to their episodes. Whitelist plays only the picked items and Blacklist plays everything else. Search a show, movie, episode, music video, or home video by name to pick it.

Age ratings

Limit which ratings air, optionally by time of day. No blocks means anything airs. On overlap, the lowest min and max win.

Placeholder blocks

A placeholder block takes the channel off air for its window. The guide shows one program by the block's guide name and the channel loops a card with your message. Whatever is airing when the window opens is cut there, and the loop goes on when it ends.
The Custom style plays an image, GIF, or video from a path on the server. A GIF or video loops cleanly when it is exactly 10 seconds and ends where it starts, and its sound is not played. In the message, {time} becomes the time the channel resumes and {contentName} what it resumes with. Leave the title or message empty to leave it off.
Items starting this close to a window change must fit both windows. Set it to your longest content. 0 turns it off.

Review scores

Only include content rated at least this by the audience, on a 0 to 10 scale, like 7.5 for a best-of channel. 0 allows everything. Content with no community rating is dropped.
Only include content with at least this critic score, on a 0 to 100 scale. 0 allows everything. Content with no critic rating is dropped.

Metadata

Limit the channel to certain production years, like a 90s channel. Enter years and ranges separated by commas, for example 1990-1999 or 1985, 1999, 2003. Leave blank to allow every year. For shows this uses each episode's year, so a long-running series contributes only its episodes from those years.
Limit the channel to these studios or networks, like an HBO channel. Leave empty to allow all. For shows this also matches the series' studio.
Limit the channel to content featuring these people, like a favourite director or actor. Search by name to add. Leave empty to allow everyone.

Playback

Bakes subtitles into the picture for everyone. Forced only burns the forced track, or the full track when the audio is in another language, while Always burns them on everything.
