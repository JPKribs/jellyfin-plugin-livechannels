Troubleshooting

Finds how many streams this server can encode at once using the real channel pipeline. Each round encodes a minute of the test item with one more stream than the last, until a stream drops below 30 fps. That count is your Maximum Concurrent Streams. It runs for a few minutes, loads the server heavily, and needs all channel streams stopped first.
Search for one movie or episode to test with. Something demanding like 4K or HDR gives the most honest number. This choice is not saved.
Rebuilds every channel schedule, the guide, and every channel's buffer cards from your current settings. Use it to clear a stale schedule, like after changing a channel's genres or filters.
