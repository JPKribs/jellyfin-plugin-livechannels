Age ratings

Limit which ratings air, optionally by time of day. No blocks means anything airs. On overlap, the lowest min and max win. A placeholder block takes the channel off air for its window: the guide shows one program by the block's name and the channel loops a card with your message. Whatever is airing when the window opens is cut there, and when the window ends the channel goes on with the next item in its loop.
